# Zava Credit Union - Credit Officer Lending Authority

Version 2.1. Owner: Consumer Lending. Audience: Credit Officers.

## Individual approval limits

| Role | Unsecured loans | Secured auto loans |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Credit Officer | Up to $25,000 | Up to $60,000 |
| Senior Credit Officer | Up to $35,000 | Up to $90,000 |

## Escalation

- Requests above a Senior Credit Officer's limit go to the Credit Committee.
- The Credit Committee meets every Tuesday and decides within two business days of the meeting.
- A Credit Officer may never approve their own loan or the loan of a relative.
- Unsecured amounts above $25,000 still need the second underwriter sign-off required by the Consumer Lending Policy, even for a Senior Credit Officer.
