# Zava Credit Union - Consumer Lending Policy

Version 4.2. Owner: Consumer Lending. Audience: Credit Officers and underwriters.

## Unsecured personal loans

- Maximum amount: $35,000 per member.
- Minimum credit score: 660.
- Maximum debt-to-income (DTI) ratio: 43 percent, calculated including the new loan payment.
- Loans above $25,000 require sign-off from a second underwriter before funding.
- Maximum term: 60 months.

## Auto loans

| Vehicle | Maximum loan-to-value | Minimum score | Maximum term |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| New | 100 percent of invoice, plus tax and title | 700 | 72 months |
| Used | 90 percent of retail book value | 660 | 60 months |

## Exceptions

- Any exception to a hard limit needs written approval from the VP of Consumer Lending.
- The approval must be recorded in the loan file within one business day.
- No exception may be granted for a DTI ratio above 50 percent.
