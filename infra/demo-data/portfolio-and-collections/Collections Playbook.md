# Zava Credit Union - Collections Playbook

Version 3.0. Owner: Portfolio Management. Audience: Portfolio Analysts and collections staff.

## Contact timeline

| Days past due | Required action |
| --- | --- |
| 5 | Automated reminder by text and email |
| 30 | Outreach call within two business days |
| 60 | Hardship outreach call before any additional fee is assessed |
| 90 | Escalate the account to a collections lead |
| 120 | Charge-off review by the Collections Manager |

## Fees and hardship plans

- The late fee is $25 flat.
- The late fee is waived once per member every 12 months.
- Hardship payment plans run up to 12 months.
- A hardship plan must collect at least 50 percent of the regular monthly payment.
- Only a collections lead can approve a plan longer than 12 months.
