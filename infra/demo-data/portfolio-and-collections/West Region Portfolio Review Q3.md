# Zava Credit Union - West Region Portfolio Review, Q3

Prepared by Portfolio Management for the West region.

## Headline numbers

- Total loan balances: $412 million.
- 30-plus day delinquency: 2.9 percent, up from 2.4 percent in Q2.
- 60-plus day delinquency: 1.4 percent.
- Auto loans are 38 percent of balances.
- Largest single-employer concentration: 6.2 percent of auto balances.

## Watchlist rules

- A branch goes on the watchlist when its 60-plus day delinquency rate is above 1.5 percent.
- An employer goes on the watchlist when it represents more than 8 percent of auto balances.
