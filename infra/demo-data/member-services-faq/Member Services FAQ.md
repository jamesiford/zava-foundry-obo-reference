# Zava Credit Union - Member Services FAQ

Shared with all staff.

## Branches and hours

- Branches are open Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM local time.
- Branches are open Saturday, 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM local time.

## Wires and cards

- The cutoff for outgoing domestic wires is 3:30 PM Eastern on business days. Later requests go out the next business day.
- The ATM daily withdrawal limit is $500.
- The debit card daily purchase limit is $2,500.
- Report a lost or stolen debit card on 1-800-555-0142, available 24 hours a day.
- The first replacement debit card is free. Later replacements cost $5.

## Share certificates

- Early withdrawal on a certificate of 12 months or less costs 90 days of dividends.
- Early withdrawal on a longer certificate costs 180 days of dividends.
